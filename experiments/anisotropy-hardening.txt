The `fit aspect` columns answer whether a round START biases the fitted ANSWER
toward roundness: compare the circle rows against the oriented ones. They do
not -- every start that reaches the good basin reports the same strongly
anisotropic ellipsoid, so a circular start is a scaffold the solver discards.

The `reached the good basin` counts are the robustness measure that matters: a
family whose BEST member wins but whose other members diverge is not safe as
the only family in the dictionary. See anisotropy-hardening.md and
dev/robust-init-notes.md.
